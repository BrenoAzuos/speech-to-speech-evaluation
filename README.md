Speech-to-Speech AI Evaluation

About This Project

This repository presents original demonstration examples of Speech-to-Speech (S2S) AI evaluation, focusing on audio quality, conversational naturalness, pronunciation, fluency, and response relevance.

The goal is to demonstrate structured evaluation, critical listening, model comparison, and quality assessment skills.

Skills Demonstrated

- Speech-to-Speech Evaluation
- Audio Quality Assessment
- Conversational Naturalness
- Pronunciation Evaluation
- Speech Fluency
- Response Relevance
- Model Comparison
- Critical Thinking
- Quality Assurance

Evaluation Criteria

1. Audio Quality (AQ): Is the audio clear and free from major defects?
2. Naturalness: Does the speech sound human and conversational?
3. Pronunciation: Are words pronounced correctly and clearly?
4. Fluency: Is the speech smooth, with appropriate pacing?
5. Conversational Relevance: Does the response address the user's intent?
6. Overall Preference: Which response provides the better overall experience?

Quality Assurance

A consistent evaluation process should include:

- Listening carefully to the complete audio.
- Identifying major and minor audio defects.
- Assessing naturalness and pronunciation.
- Checking conversational relevance.
- Applying evaluation guidelines consistently.
- Providing clear and objective justifications.
